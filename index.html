short-video-posting

short-video-posting is a private, single-operator tool used by MG Group to publish short videos to its own YouTube channels (Enechiku and Tokyo City Daikou).

The tool runs on the operator's own computer. After the channel owner grants permission once through Google's sign-in screen, the tool uploads video files, titles and descriptions to those channels using the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and no other person's account is accessed.
