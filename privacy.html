Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

short-video-posting is a private tool operated by MG Group to publish short videos to its own YouTube channels.

Data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to access the owner's YouTube channels for the sole purpose of uploading videos and reading basic channel information needed to confirm where an upload went.

How data is stored and used

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are used only to perform the uploads described above. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Once revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
